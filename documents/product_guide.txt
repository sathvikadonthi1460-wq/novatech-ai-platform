NovaTech Product Guide

NovaTech offers three subscription plans: Basic, Standard, and Premium.

The Basic plan includes access to core platform features and email support.

The Standard plan includes all Basic features, advanced analytics, and priority customer support.

The Premium plan includes all Standard features, dedicated account support, advanced reporting, and early access to new product features.

Customers can upgrade their subscription plan at any time.

Downgrades take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

Product features may vary depending on account type and subscription level.
