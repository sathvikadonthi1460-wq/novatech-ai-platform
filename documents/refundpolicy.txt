NovaTech Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of their initial subscription purchase.

Annual subscription refunds are prorated based on the unused portion of the subscription.

Refund requests must be submitted through the customer support portal.

Refunds are normally processed within 5 to 10 business days after approval.

Customers who have received service credits may have the credit amount deducted from their eligible refund.
