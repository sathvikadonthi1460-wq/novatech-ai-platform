NovaTech Customer Retention Policy

NovaTech offers subscription-based technology products to individual and business customers.

Customers who have been subscribed for more than 12 months are eligible for a loyalty discount of up to 15 percent when renewing an annual subscription.

Customers considering cancellation may be offered a retention discount of up to 20 percent depending on their subscription plan and account history.

Customers experiencing technical problems should first be directed to the technical support team.

Customers with unresolved technical issues may qualify for service credits depending on the severity and duration of the issue.

Customer service representatives should not promise discounts above the approved limits without manager authorization.

High-risk customers are defined as customers with a high likelihood of cancellation based on recent usage patterns, support interactions, payment problems, or other behavioral indicators.

Retention offers should be personalized based on customer history and should not be automatically offered to every customer.

Customer retention actions should be documented in the customer relationship management system.
